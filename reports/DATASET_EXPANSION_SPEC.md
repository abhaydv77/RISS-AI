## D. Labeling Strategy

### Phase 1: Initial Diverse Subset (2,000 pairs)

Select 2,000 pairs (20% of total) for initial annotation, stratified as follows:

| Category | Count | % of Phase 1 | Selection Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positive examples (good) | 400 | 20% | Strong niche + audience + geography + platform match |
| Borderline examples (maybe) | 600 | 30% | 2+ dimensions match, 1–2 partial/weak |
| Hard negatives (poor) | 600 | 30% | Clear mismatch on 2+ dimensions |
| Cross-geography mismatches | 200 | 10% | Creator base/audience outside brand target locations |
| Platform mismatches | 100 | 5% | No platform overlap between creator and brand |
| Budget mismatches | 50 | 2.5% | Creator rate card far outside brand budget |
| Creator-size mismatches | 50 | 2.5% | Follower count far outside brand min/max range |

### Phase 2: Model-Guided Expansion (3,000 pairs)

After training initial model on Phase 1:
- Select 3,000 pairs where model uncertainty is highest
- Focus on pairs near decision boundaries
- Include pairs with conflicting signals (strong niche but weak geography, etc.)

### Phase 3: Full Coverage (5,000 pairs)

Label remaining 5,000 pairs to complete all 10,000 combinations.

### Sampling Approach per Brand

For each of the 50 brands, select 40 creators (2,000 total) using:

1. **Rule-based pre-filtering**: Use `feature_engineering.py` to compute match scores
2. **Stratified sampling**: Divide creators into deciles by match score, sample proportionally
3. **Dimension-specific quotas**: Ensure each brand includes:
   - 5 creators with strong niche match
   - 5 creators with geography mismatch
   - 5 creators with platform mismatch
   - 5 creators with budget/size mismatch
   - 20 creators with mixed/partial matches

### Quality Controls

- Each annotator sees brand + creator profiles side-by-side
- Use `ANNOTATION_GUIDE.md` criteria consistently
- Inter-annotator agreement check on 10% sample
- Discrepancy resolution by third annotator
